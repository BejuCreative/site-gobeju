The engine

How the engine works.

Seven stages. One team. From a raw recording to a booked call, this is what happens to every piece of content you give us. Scroll, and watch it run.

The screens are illustrations of how the system works.

  1. 01Mine

    We mine your best moments.

    Every recording you send is raw ore. We watch all of it, minute by minute, not a skim. We pull every line that could stop a thumb mid-scroll.

    Then we rank each moment on three things. How hard the hook lands. Whether there's a real story. Whether it pays off at the end. Only the strongest moments make the cut.

    • Every recording watched end to end
    • Hooks pulled and ranked by strength
    • Story and payoff scored on each moment
    • The shortlist mapped to your content plan

Questions.

Anything else? Ask us on the call.

How much does it cost?

We quote after a short call. The price depends on how many Shorts you need, which platforms we post on, and how much of the funnel and website we build for you.

What do I have to do?

Record and approve. Talk to the camera, or send recordings you already have: podcasts, lives, webinars, workshops. We handle the strategy, the edits, the posting, the funnel and the reporting.

Do I approve things before they go out?

Yes. You see the plan, the Shorts and the funnel before anything goes live, and you can ask for changes at any point.

Which platforms do you post on?

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn. Each one gets its own caption, hashtags and cover, posted natively rather than copied across.

Do people have to follow me to get the free gift?

No. Everyone who comments the keyword gets the gift. We invite people to follow along, but it's never a condition.

I don't have a free gift or an email list yet. Is that a problem?

No. We help you shape a free gift from what you already teach, then build the DM keyword, the sign-up and the email series around it.

Do you need access to my accounts?

Yes, to post for you. We ask for the lowest access that lets us post, and you can remove it at any time.

Is my footage kept private?

Yes. Footage stays private. Upload folders are shared only with you, and raw files are deleted once the edits are approved.

How do I start?

Book a 15-minute call. We look at your channels together and tell you what we would run first.
